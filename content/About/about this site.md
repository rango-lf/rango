### colophon

I use [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), a digital note-taking tool, for drafting and writing all of the content you see here. I built this site with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), which is a free(!) static-site generator for Markdown content. Then, I host everything on [Github](https://github.com/rango-lf/rango)and deploy it to Vercel, where you are currently viewing this.
