## What is Industrial Engineering?
Broadly speaking, industrial engineering (IE) focuses on the design, implementation, management, and optimization of systems. We aim to reduce waste, streamline operations, improve performance and more within processes, organisations, and systems.

Industrial engineering shares significant overlap with [operations research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_research), [systems engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering), and [management science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_science). Systems engineering and industrial engineering are often treated as synonymous with each other (i.e. ISE -- industrial and systems engineering).

Specifically within the University of Toronto's IE program, particular focus is given to information engineering, artificial intelligence, human factors, and operations research. 
## What Do You Learn?
The courses in this discipline tend to focus on programming, data science, applied mathematics, and ergonomics.
