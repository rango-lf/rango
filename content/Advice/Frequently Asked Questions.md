## Admissions Questions
#### "What Average/ECs Do I Need for (blank)?"
We get this question on the Discord server and Reddit forum more times than I can count. Truthfully, we as students are not equipped to answer this question concretely. Admission into the UofT Engineering program depends on several factors: your grades, ECs, online interview, but also the opinions and biases of whoever is reviewing your admission profile. I have seen people with spectacular grades and ECs get rejected, whereas those with lower grades are admitted. At a certain point when you meet the competitive average, your admission decision can truly be up to RNG.

If you would like to see the mean admissions averages from previous years, please refer to the following:
