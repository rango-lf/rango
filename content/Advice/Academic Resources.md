## Course Resources
- [Samar Q.'s course resources](https://www.samarq.org/notes) - notes, past tests, and study materials for Core 8 and upper-year ECE courses
- [Arnav's notes](https://arnav-patil-12.github.io/notes/year-one/) for most of the Core 8 courses
- [Matthew's ESP 1 notes](https://mattkongnotes.vercel.app/uof-t-1st-year/fall-2024-semester/esp-1/esp-1-studying/) - a quick reference handbook for important APS111/112 concepts and methods
- ["Golden List"](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Vvgq8Mac_tG_wHCdZGfBGMb91uwZMqLjhaFvVGb4GM4/edit) - notes, slides, and videos for Core 8 courses. Watch Shai Cohen's math lectures!
- [Skule Courses](https://courses.skule.ca/) - repository of syllabi, quizzes, tests, cheatsheets, notes, and exams
- [F!rosh Anti-Calendar](https://courses.skule.ca/anticalendar/frosh) - reviews and tips for each course; some info may be outdated
- [First-year textbooks Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17R2Jvm-g4l6a4w681e8LFXGSkAWMDTGg) - Please check your syllabus for correct edition
- [Past tests and exams](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/burbulla/detailed/) for MAT188, MAT187, MAT186
- [Guide to UofT course codes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1on4LkDt-TJiRzdiBEbudW1rjmzBU3TZcCoz8O7cQUMo/edit)
- [Paul's Online Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/) - tutorials, practice problems, and cheat sheets for calculus
## Academic Services
- [engSuccess Mentors](https://undergrad.engineering.utoronto.ca/skule-life/the-engineering-portal/) - upper-year peer mentors for time management, note-taking, and academic challenges
- [Guide to First Year](https://firstyear.engineering.utoronto.ca/preparing-for-university/) - academic support info, pre-school prep, summer learning
- [Engineering Academic Calendar](https://engineering.calendar.utoronto.ca/) - program requirements, courses, regulations
- [Engineering Student Portal](https://undergrad.engineering.utoronto.ca/skule-life/the-engineering-portal/) - petitions, program transfers, letters
- [Skule Tutors](https://undergrad.engineering.utoronto.ca/skule-life/the-engineering-portal/) - PhD and MASc students for paid tutoring; overseen directly by EngSoc
- [Guided Engineering Academic Review Sessions (GEARS)](https://undergrad.engineering.utoronto.ca/first-year-office-2/first-year-updates-deadlines/guided-engineering-academic-review-sessions/) - drop-in study sessions for first-year courses; led by upper-years
