>[!info] This guide was specifically written with University of Toronto Engineering students in mind. This is also based on my personal experience, and may not be universally applicable. 
## So You Want to Get Into Research
- The University of Toronto is the top university in Canada for research; opportunities to get involved are abundant
- I got a summer research position in my first year in the Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering
	- My project was related to maternal and infant health outcomes
- Benefits:
	- Explore topics that interest you and gain a deeper understanding of them
	- Develop collaboration, communication, critical thinking, and other professional and academic skills
	- Determine what you're passionate in
	- Networking opportunities with faculty; could provide letters of recommendation and industry connections in the future
	- Pad your CV and resume
	- Earn money
	- Research work counts towards your [practical experience requirement](https://engineering.calendar.utoronto.ca/curriculum#practical).
Being the top university in Canada for research, the University of Toronto puts you in an excellent position to dip your toes into academia. 
## Cold Email Example
**Subject: Inquiry About Summer Research Opportunities in [Professor’s Area of Expertise]**

Dear Prof/Dr. [name],

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is [name] and I am a [major] student at UofT. I am deeply interested in [specific area of research], and I am eager to gain hands-on experience in research that aligns with your work on [mention a specific project, paper, or area from their research].

I have been developing a strong foundation in [relevant coursework or skills, e.g., 3D printing, CAD modeling, MATLAB programming], and I am highly motivated to contribute to your team’s ongoing projects. I am particularly drawn to your research because [briefly explain why—impact, innovation, or personal interest].

Would you be open to discussing potential opportunities for me to assist in your lab during [term & year]? I am flexible regarding the scope of work and willing to learn quickly to meet your expectations. I can provide my resume and transcript upon request.

Thank you for considering my inquiry.

Best regards,
[name & pronouns]
[email]
